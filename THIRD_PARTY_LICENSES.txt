MorphoDiTa runtime, English model, and optional offline dictionaries
==================================

This extension combines components with different licenses. The project's MIT
license covers its own wrapper, UI, and integration code. It does not relicense
the MorphoDiTa library, the English model, or the dictionary data.

Optional offline dictionaries: adapted WikDict / Wiktionary data, CC BY-SA 4.0.
No word dictionary data is bundled in an installation ZIP.
Attribution, source URLs, and changes: third-party/WikDict-NOTICE.txt.
Pinned source hashes: dictionary-sources.json in the source repository.
Download pack hashes and source URLs: dictionary-catalog.js.
License text: third-party/Dictionary-CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt.
